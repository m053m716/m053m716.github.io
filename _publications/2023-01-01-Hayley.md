excerpt: 'Investigates whether peripheral sensory stimulation activates premotor cortex following primary motor cortex injury in rats. Neural responses to mechanical forepaw stimulation were recorded four weeks post-ischemic lesion in seven rats. Post-ischemic functional connectivity emerged between premotor and somatosensory cortex, and stimulation of premotor regions modulated sensory responses—suggesting that premotor–somatosensory coupling is a substrate for sensorimotor recovery after stroke.'
