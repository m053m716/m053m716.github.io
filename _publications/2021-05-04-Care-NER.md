excerpt: 'Acquired brain injuries, such as stroke, are a major cause of long-term disability worldwide. We investigated how neuronal activity is modified following an injury to primary motor cortex and in response to an activity-dependent stimulation (ADS) protocol, recording spike activity in the rostral forelimb area and somatosensory cortex of brain-injured anesthetized rats. An ischemic injury in M1 led to an overall increase in spike activity within RFA and S1, and subsequent treatment with ADS promoted the generation of synchronized patterns in both areas.'
