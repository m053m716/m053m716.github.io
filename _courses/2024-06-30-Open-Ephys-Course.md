### Open Ephys Course ###
I maintain **[Open Ephys course materials](https://about.murphylab.net/OE-ephys-course)** for teaching practical electrophysiology workflows and related tooling.

The course is intended as a compact reference for getting oriented with Open Ephys-style acquisition, data handling, and experimental interfaces.
