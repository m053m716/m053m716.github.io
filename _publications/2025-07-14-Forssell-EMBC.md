excerpt: 'Demonstrates precision motor-cortex targeting using a 64-electrode high-density surface array delivering 100 µs pulses at 50–150 mA. Moving the injected electric field by sub-centimeter distances measurably changes the activation profile of arm and hand muscles as monitored by EMG. The results suggest that steerable transcranial electrical stimulation could substitute for invasive stimulation in some clinical contexts and enhance intraoperative muscle-function monitoring.'
