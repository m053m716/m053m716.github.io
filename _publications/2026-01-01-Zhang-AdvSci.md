A comprehensive framework incorporating nonlinear physics effects predicts that properly engineered MENPs could enable deep brain and cortical neuromodulation and recording with submillimeter spatial resolution and millisecond temporal precision, potentially offering clinically viable BCIs without implanted electrodes or genetic modification.'
